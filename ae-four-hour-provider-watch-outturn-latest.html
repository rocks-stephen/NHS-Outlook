Trajectory-watch rule. Six actual months are compared with one path forecast before the six-month window. The sparkline begins at the shared observed starting value, then shows how actual performance diverged from that fixed path. The displayed gap is the latest actual minus the fixed forecast for that same month. A signal requires a material latest-month gap and at least five of six months in that direction. These are screening prompts—not league tables, causal estimates or judgements of provider quality.

Reference: 95% constitutional standard.
