How to read this. Outlooks are one-month-ahead forecasts made only with information available before release. The 80% range reflects historic forecast errors. The target-date column compares the projected value with the stated threshold for the indicated month. The chance is provisional and model-implied, estimated from the projected 80% range assuming a Normal distribution; it is not a validated probability of policy success or of sustaining the standard. Provider signals compare actual performance with a path fixed six months earlier; they are prompts for review, not rankings.
